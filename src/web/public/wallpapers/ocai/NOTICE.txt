06 and 07 are not from the site: they were generated with Codex image
generation to the site's DESIGN.md (navy, sun and paper inks only), with
navy kept to the edges and under about a third of the frame, since the
theme's terminal text is navy and has to stay readable over the image.

TRANSFORMATIONS APPLIED (ImageMagick 7, same recipe as retro-82/NOTICE.txt)
  Full image  magick <src> -auto-orient -strip -resize '1920x1920>' \
                     -define webp:method=6 -quality 82 <id>.webp
  Thumbnail   magick <src> -auto-orient -strip -resize '320x320>' \
                     -define webp:method=6 -quality 75 thumbs/<id>.webp
